Who we are

ANTB ORIGINALS is a creative and technology studio. For the purposes of this policy, ANTB ORIGINALS is the party responsible for deciding how and why your personal data is processed (the data controller) in connection with this website.

You can reach us at luisantoniotinocoberroteran@antboriginals.com with any question about this policy or about your personal data.

Data we collect

We only collect personal data that you choose to share with us. This site does not require an account and does not run advertising or analytics scripts.

  • Contact form: the name, email address and message you enter when you write to us.
  • Standard technical logs: our hosting provider and font provider (see Third parties) may automatically record standard access data such as IP address, browser type and request timestamps, as most web infrastructure does.

How we use it

We use the information submitted through the contact form only to respond to your message and to manage the resulting conversation or project. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where required, our legal basis for this processing is your informed consent, given when you voluntarily submit the form.

Costa Rica: Law No. 8968 & PRODHAB

The processing of personal data in Costa Rica is governed primarily by Law No. 8968, Protección de la Persona frente al tratamiento de sus datos personales, and supervised by the Agencia de Protección de Datos de los Habitantes (PRODHAB). We handle personal data submitted through this site in line with the principles that law establishes, including:

  • Informed consent: we only process the data you knowingly and voluntarily provide.
  • Proportionality: we collect only the data needed to respond to your enquiry, nothing beyond that.
  • Security: we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the data you share with us against loss, misuse or unauthorized access.
  • Confidentiality: your data is not disclosed to third parties except where this policy says otherwise or where the law requires it.

Your rights

Under Law No. 8968, you may exercise the following rights over your personal data at any time:

  • Access: ask us what personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: ask us to correct inaccurate or outdated data.
  • Suppression (deletion): ask us to delete your personal data where we have no legitimate reason to keep it.

To exercise any of these rights, write to luisantoniotinocoberroteran@antboriginals.com. If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may also file a complaint with PRODHAB.

Data retention

We keep messages sent through the contact form only for as long as needed to address your enquiry and for a reasonable period afterward for our own records, unless you ask us to delete it sooner or the law requires a longer period.

Third parties

This site relies on a small number of external services to function:

  • Hosting: the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes standard web server logs under GitHub's own privacy policy.
  • Fonts: typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which may process your IP address to serve the correct font files.
  • Contact form: depending on how the form is configured, your message may be delivered directly to our inbox by email, or routed through a form-processing service, before reaching us.

We do not otherwise share your personal data with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Cookies

This site does not set its own cookies or use analytics or advertising trackers. Loading fonts and hosting the page may involve standard technical requests to the providers listed above, which is normal for any website and is not used to track you across other sites.

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general business and creative audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or in applicable law. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last revised.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to access, correct or delete your data, can be sent to luisantoniotinocoberroteran@antboriginals.com.
