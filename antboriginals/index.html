Ownership is part of the work

Every original we create is treated as intellectual property from the start. We manage copyrights, trademarks and licensing agreements, keep clear records of authorship, and structure how work is published, produced or licensed to others.

Artists, designers and brands keep clear rights over what they create, and a clear path to earn from it.
